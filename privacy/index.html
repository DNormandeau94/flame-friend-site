Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Flame Friend collects no data. Nothing about you or your child leaves your phone.

The microphone

Flame Friend uses the microphone only while the candle is on screen, to measure how loud a blow is: a single number, many times a second.

  • Sound is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • To measure loudness, the iPhone's audio recorder writes a short temporary file as a side effect. The app deletes it right away and never reads or keeps it.
  • You can switch to "Taps only" in the app's parent area, or turn the microphone off in iPhone Settings → Flame Friend.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps a few things on your phone so it remembers them next time:

  • your settings (mode, sensitivity, theme, language and so on);
  • the results of "Teach Puff your blow": loudness levels and blow lengths (numbers, not sound);
  • a simple history: the date and time of each session, the mode, and how many times the flame went out. It's kept for up to one year;
  • whether Premium was bought.

All of this stays on your phone and is deleted when you delete the app. We don't receive any of it. (If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include it in your own backup, as it does for any app.)

What leaves the phone, only when you choose

  • History export: you can export the history as a spreadsheet file (CSV) with the iPhone share sheet. You choose where it goes.
  • Links: this website opens in your browser when you tap a link in the app.

Both are behind a simple grown-up question, as are the iPhone Settings link and purchases.

Purchases

The one-time Premium purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether Premium was bought on this Apple Account, so it can unlock the themes.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

There's no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind, and no third-party code that collects data. The app doesn't connect to any server of ours (we don't have one). Its only internet connection is to Apple, for purchases.

Children

Flame Friend is made for children 5 and under, used with a grown-up. It doesn't collect personal information from children, or from anyone else.

This website

These pages use no cookies, trackers, analytics, or outside fonts or scripts. They're hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

What is Flame Friend?

A calm app for toddlers: your child blows at the phone to put out Puff the candle's flame, gets a gentle reward, and the flame lights again. There's also a guided Calm Breathing mode and a Birthday cake with 1 to 10 candles.

Getting started

  • Hold the phone upright, with its bottom edge toward your child's mouth: that's where the microphone is.
  • The parent area opens when you press and hold the grown-up icon (top right) for 2 seconds.
  • Tapping the flame puts it out too, for children who can't blow yet.

Puff doesn’t react when my child blows

  • Check that the microphone is allowed: iPhone Settings → Flame Friend → Microphone.
  • In the parent area, choose Sensitivity → Extra easy, or use "Teach Puff your blow" so Puff learns your child's blow.
  • Aim at the bottom edge of the phone.

Puff goes out when we talk

In the parent area, choose Sensitivity → Normal or Noisy room. Loud talking right next to the phone can still count sometimes: Flame Friend prefers catching a soft blow over ignoring it.

Premium

Classic Puff and every mode are free. Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks all the themes, and every theme added later, at no extra cost. No subscription. It's shared with your family through Family Sharing.

How to restore a purchase

  1. Make sure the iPhone is signed in to the same Apple Account that bought Premium.
  2. Open the parent area (hold the grown-up icon for 2 seconds) → Themes → Restore purchases.
  3. Answer the grown-up question. You'll see "Your purchase is restored."

After reinstalling the app, Premium usually comes back by itself the first time the app is opened with an internet connection.

Refunds
